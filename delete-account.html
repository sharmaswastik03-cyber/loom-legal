Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your Loom account and all associated data at any time. Choose the method that works best for you.

Recommended

Delete directly in the app

The fastest way to delete your account is from within the Loom app. Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

  1. Open the Loom app on your phone
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom right)
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted
Alternative

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us. We will process your request within 7 days.

Email deletion request →
What gets deleted
  • Your account and profile information (name, email, age, gender)
  • All wardrobe data including scanned garment images and AI-generated tags
  • Saved outfits and outfit history
  • AI Stylist chat history
  • All schedule and occasion data

What may be retained
  • Anonymised, aggregated analytics data that cannot be linked back to you
  • Data required to be retained for legal or compliance purposes (retained for up to 12 months)
